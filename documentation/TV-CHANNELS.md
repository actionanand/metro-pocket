# TV channels

The client recognizes JioTV+, Tata Play, Airtel Digital TV, Sun Direct, Dish TV, d2h, TACTV, TCCL/SCV, KAL Cables, VK Digital, ACT Digital, SITI, ACN Cable, Hathway, GTPL Hathway, DEN, Asianet Digital, KCCL and Fastway as data-provider identities. Actual numbers are never in client source.

TV packs contain providers, channels and mappings. A mapping can be scoped by country, state, city, area/head-end, network revision and verified date. Search supports name, alias and number; region and provider filters prevent cable results from being treated as globally valid. Missing mappings are displayed as unavailable, never fabricated.
