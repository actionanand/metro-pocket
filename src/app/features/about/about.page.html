About

METROPOCKET

Useful information, kept private.

MetroPocket keeps metro routes, TV channel information and your own train schedules available offline. Metro and TV use public data packs; train schedules are imported and managed privately on your device.

Privacy

No account, analytics, advertising, GPS, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications or shared-storage permission.

Data sources

Metro and TV records are supplied by separately released data packs. Offline estimates are not live service information. Train timetables are user-managed schedules, not live or automatically verified.

Offline first

Installed data remains on your device or browser. Train schedules and backups are processed locally, without uploads, API calls or location access. Export raw SQLite Train backups from Settings before clearing storage or changing devices. Restore replaces existing Train data after confirmation, without a password or encryption. Internet is needed for the initial web load and optional public-pack updates.
