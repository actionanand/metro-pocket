About

METROPOCKET

Useful information, kept private.

MetroPocket is an offline-first route and channel lookup utility. It downloads public, versioned data packs only when you choose to install them.

Privacy

No account, analytics, advertising, GPS, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications or shared-storage permission.

Data sources

Metro and TV records are supplied by separately released data packs. Offline estimates are not live service information.

Offline first

Installed data remains on your device or browser. Internet access is used only for optional updates and downloads.
